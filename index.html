About This Calculator
Our calculator is created to help students easily find their GPA in a simple and accurate way. It follows the official grading system used in Nepal’s education system and is suitable for Grade 12 learners. Whether you are from Science or Management stream, it includes both theory and practical marks to give you a clear and correct result of your academic performance.
  • certified National Examination Board (NEB) grading system.
  • Clear subject-wise breakdown of grades and performance.
  • Instant gpa calculation according to your stream.
  • Fast and reliable to calculate GPA.
  • 100% accurate GPA calculation.
  • Works smoothly on desktop and mobile devices.
